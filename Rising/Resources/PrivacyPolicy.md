# Rising - Privacy Policy

**Last Updated: April 2026**

## Overview

Rising is designed with privacy as a core principle. Your financial data stays on your device.

## Data We Collect

### On-Device Data (Local Storage Only)
- **Goals & Savings Data**: Goal names, target amounts, current progress, deadlines, and descriptions you create
- **Deposit History**: Amounts, dates, and optional notes for deposits toward your goals
- **Property Information**: Addresses, prices, and notes for properties you're considering
- **Agent Contacts**: Names, phone numbers, emails, and notes for real estate agents
- **Milestones**: Track progress milestones for your savings goals
- **App Preferences**: Theme selection (dark/light/system) stored locally

### No Personal Financial Accounts
Rising does NOT connect to your bank accounts, credit cards, or investment accounts. We do not store your SSN, bank account numbers, or any financial credentials.

### Analytics (Future)
If analytics are enabled in a future version, they would only collect:
- App feature usage (which screens you visit)
- Crash reports
- Aggregate anonymous performance data

No personal data, financial information, or individual savings amounts would ever be transmitted.

## How We Use Data

### Your Goals
We use your goal data to:
- Calculate progress toward your targets
- Generate personalized savings tips (on-device, using local context)
- Track your savings milestones

### AI Savings Tips (R8 Feature)
The AI savings tips feature uses only on-device data:
- Current goal progress percentage
- Recent deposit patterns (frequency, amounts)
- Days remaining until deadlines

No personal data is sent to external AI services.

## Data Storage

All your data is stored locally on your device using SQLite, a local database. This data is NOT synchronized to cloud servers.

### Data Deletion
You can delete all your data at any time by:
1. Opening Settings within Rising
2. Tapping "Reset All Data"
3. Confirming deletion

Alternatively, deleting the app removes all data.

## Third-Party Services

### SQLite.swift (Local Database)
- **Purpose**: Local data storage
- **Privacy**: All data stays on your device

### Plaid Integration (Future - Not Yet Implemented)
If Plaid bank sync is enabled in a future version:
- Plaid's privacy policy would apply
- Bank credentials never touch our servers
- Read-only access to transaction data

## Children's Privacy

Rising is not designed for or marketed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

## Changes to This Policy

If this privacy policy changes, we will update the "Last Updated" date and, for significant changes, notify users through the app.

## Your Rights

You have full control over your data:
- **Access**: View all your saved goals, deposits, and settings within the app
- **Correction**: Edit any goal or deposit information
- **Deletion**: Delete individual items or reset all data
- **Portability**: Export your data (future feature)

## Contact Us

For privacy concerns or data questions:

- In-App: Settings → Privacy
